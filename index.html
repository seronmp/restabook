Booking automation

From guest message to confirmed table.

The animation shows the lifecycle of a WhatsApp request, from intake through availability validation to the restaurant dashboard.

  1. Step 01

    Capture the request

    A guest sends a WhatsApp message with a date, time, and party size. If details are missing, the bot asks for them.

  2. Step 02

    Parse and validate

    The service resolves the requested date and time, collects the guest name, and checks that the request is valid.

  3. Step 03

    Check table availability

    SmartTable finds a suitable table and rechecks availability when creating the booking to avoid overlapping reservations.

  4. Step 04

    Confirm and sync

    The reservation is saved and appears in the management dashboard alongside the restaurant's other bookings.

Platform capabilities

The operational tools your team needs.

Configurable floor plan

Configure rooms, walls, table positions, size, and seating capacity. Visualize availability by selected date and service period.

Reservation operations

Manage bookings from the calendar and floor plan, review guest details, track expected covers, and handle cancellations or restores.

WhatsApp integration

Process guest requests through a WhatsApp bot that gathers missing information, checks table availability, and writes confirmed reservations to the booking system.

Built for daily operations

A connected workflow for front-of-house teams.

SmartTable brings floor configuration, reservations, and table availability into one browser-based workspace. Staff can manage room layouts and bookings from a shared operational view instead of switching between disconnected tools.

The interface supports Italian and German, responsive layouts for desktop and tablet, and multi-restaurant selection for authorized users.
